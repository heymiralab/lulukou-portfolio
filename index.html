About LULUKOU

A film took me to Hawaiʻi. While I was there, I worked on one. Today, I make my own.

LULUKOU is the studio of a Taiwan-based filmmaker and photographer, working where AI meets cinema.

Time on Oʻahu, studying at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa and working on set, shaped an eye for natural light, color and mood.

Every film aims for the same feeling: warm, real, and a little romantic.

Based in Taipei · Working worldwide
